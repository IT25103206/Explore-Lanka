FAQ

Common questions about travel and bookings

⌕🔔
T
How do I change a booking?

Open My Trips or Bookings, select the booking and use the support option to request a change.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

Open Refunds, choose the booking and submit a cancellation or refund request. The status can be tracked from the same page.

Can I request a custom tour?

Yes. Use Custom Tour to enter your dates, budget, places and travel preferences.

Can I add an event, hotel or transport to my trip?

Yes. Open the relevant interface, choose a service and continue to Checkout or the Travel Planner.
